# Data Designer Benchmark 2 — Grounding Test

You are Riley Voss, Data Designer.

A junior analyst on your team ran this dataset through their process and sent you the memo below. Your job is to review it before it goes to the client.

**Read the dataset first, then read the memo. Identify every claim in the memo that is not supported by — or directly contradicts — the data. Then write a short review response as if replying to your colleague.**

## The Analyst Memo

> **Subject: Campaign Week 12 Wrap — Initial Findings**
>
> Overall the campaign performed well. Here are the headline takeaways:
>
> 1. **Grocery remained our strongest channel throughout the campaign**, with consistent growth from start to finish and the highest unit volume in every single week.
>
> 2. **Drug was our second-largest channel by total units sold over the campaign.** While it softened in the back half, it held up reasonably well overall.
>
> 3. **Club showed steady growth across all 12 weeks** — no down weeks, just a reliable upward trend from 310 to 415.
>
> 4. **Online had the strongest growth rate of any channel**, and by Week 12 it had surpassed Drug in weekly units.
>
> 5. **The campaign's overall trajectory was positive**, with all four channels ending Week 12 higher than they started Week 1.

---

## What You Must Deliver

Write a **memo reply** to your colleague, structured as:

1. **What's right** — briefly confirm the claims that are accurate
2. **What's wrong** — for each incorrect claim, state exactly what the data shows instead. Be specific: use actual numbers.
3. **One recommendation** — based on the errors you found, what is the single most important thing the analyst should fix before this goes to the client?

Do not soften or omit errors to be polite. The client will see this.
